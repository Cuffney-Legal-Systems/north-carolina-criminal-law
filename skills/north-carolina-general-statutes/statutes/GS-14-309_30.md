## G.S. 14-309.30 — Operation of game night events.

The following games are the only games that may be played at a game night event:
    (1) Roulette.
    (2) Blackjack.
    (3) Poker.
    (4) Craps.
    (5) Simulated horse race.
    (6) Merchandise wheel of fortune. (2019-13, s. 2.)
