## G.S. 14-309.3 — Exempt wagering.

This Article shall not apply to:
    (1) Sports wagering lawfully conducted in compliance with Article 9 of Chapter 18C of the General Statutes.
    (2) Pari-mutuel wagering lawfully conducted in compliance with Article 10 of Chapter 18C of the General Statutes. (2023-42, s. 4(e).)
