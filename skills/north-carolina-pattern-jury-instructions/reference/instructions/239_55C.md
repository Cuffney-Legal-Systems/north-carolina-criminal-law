reckless disregard for human life in the care of a child resulting in serious

bodily injury.

First, that the defendant was [the parent of] [[a  person  providing

[care to] [supervision of]]1 the child.

Second, that at that time the child had not yet reached the child!s

sixteenth birthday.

rights and safety of others.3

Fourth,  that  the  defendant!s  [willful  conduct]  [grossly  negligent

causes  permanent  disfigurement,  a  coma,  a  permanent  or  protracted

condition that causes  extreme  pain,  or  permanent  or  protracted  loss  or

impairment of the function of any bodily member or organ, or that results

in prolonged hospitalization.

If you find from the evidence beyond a reasonable doubt that on or

If you do not so find or have a reasonable doubt as to one or more

of these things, it would be your duty to return a verdict of not guilty.
