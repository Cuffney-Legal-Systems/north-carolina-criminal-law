104.20  TESTIMONY OF INTERESTED WITNESS.

You may find that  a  witness  is  interested  in  the  outcome  of  this

trial.    You  may  take  the  witness's  interest  into  account  in  deciding

whether  to  believe  the  witness.    If  you  believe  the  testimony  of  the

witness in whole or in part, you should treat what you believe the same

as any other believable evidence.
