271.36 PERMITTING THE ALTERATION OR CHANGE OF ENGINE OR OTHER
NUMBERS ON A VEHICLE. FELONY.

The  defendant  has  been  charged  with  knowingly  permitting  the

alteration  of  the  [manufacturer's  serial  number]  [transmission  number]

[engine number] on a vehicle which he owned.

For  you  to  find  the  defendant  guilty  of  this  offense,  the  State  must

prove two things beyond a reasonable doubt:

First,  that  the  defendant  knowingly  permitted  the  [manufacturer’s

serial  number]  [transmission  number]  [engine  number]  to  be  [defaced]

[destroyed] [removed] [covered] [altered] on a vehicle.

And Second, that the defendant owned the vehicle.

If  you  find  from  the  evidence  beyond  a  reasonable  doubt  that  on  or

about  the  alleged  date  the  defendant  knowingly  permitted  the  [defacing]

[destroying]  [removing]  [covering]  [altering]  of  the  [manufacturer’s  serial]

[transmission]  [engine]  number  on  the  vehicle  that  he  owned,  it  would  be

your  duty  to  return  a  verdict  of  guilty.  If  you  do  not  so  find  or  have  a

reasonable doubt as to one or more of these things, it would be your duty to

return a verdict of not guilty.
