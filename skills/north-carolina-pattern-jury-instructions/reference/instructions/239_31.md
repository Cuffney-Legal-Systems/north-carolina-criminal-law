239.31 CONCEALMENT OF DEATH - FAILURE TO NOTIFY LAW
ENFORCEMENT OF DEATH OF CHILD OR SECRETLY BURYING CHILD.
FELONY.

The  defendant  has  been  charged  with  concealing  the  death  of  a

child.

For you to find the defendant guilty of this offense, the State must

prove two things beyond a reasonable doubt:

First, that the defendant intended to conceal the  death  of  a  child

less than 16 years of age.

Second,  that  the  defendant  [failed  to  notify  a  law  enforcement

authority  of  the  death]  [[secretly  buried]  [secretly  disposed  of]  a  dead

child!s body].

If you find from the evidence beyond a reasonable doubt that on or

about the alleged date the defendant intentionally  concealed  the  death

of  a  child  by  [failing  to  notify  law  enforcement  authority  of  the  death]

[[secretly burying] [secretly disposing of] a dead child!s body]], it would

be your duty to return a verdict of guilty. If you do not so find or have a

reasonable  doubt  as  to  one  or  more  of  these  things,  then  it  would  be

your duty to return a verdict of not guilty.
