The defendant has been charged with unlawfully hunting migratory

birds on Sunday.

For you to find the defendant guilty of this offense, the State must

prove three things beyond a reasonable doubt:

First,  that  on  or  about  the  alleged  date  the  defendant  hunted

migratory birds1 on Sunday.

And Second, that defendant hunted in violation of the law by:

a) [hunting between 9:30 A.M and 12:30 P.M.2]

b) [hunting within 500 yards of  a  place  of  religious  worship3  or

any accessory structure thereof]

c) [hunted on Sunday prior to March 1, 2018]

If you find from the evidence beyond a reasonable doubt that on or

about the alleged date, the defendant hunted migratory birds on Sunday

and that defendant [hunted between 9:30 a.m. and 12:30 p.m.] [hunted

within 500 yards of a place of religious worship or any accessory structure

thereof] [hunted on Sunday prior to March 1, 2018], it would be your duty

to return a verdict of guilty. If you do not so find or have a  reasonable

doubt as to one or both of these things, it would be your duty to return a

verdict of not guilty.
