GENERAL CRIMINAL VOLUME
JUNE 2013
------------------------------

101.25  DUTY TO ABIDE BY TRANSLATION PROVIDED IN COURT.

NOTE WELL: The following instruction should be given
whenever testimony will be received in court with the
assistance of an interpreter.

Some  testimony  may  be  given  in  ________  (insert  name  or

description of language other than English).  An interpreter will provide

a translation for you at the time that the testimony is given.  You must

rely on the translation provided by the interpreter.  Do  not  retranslate

any testimony for other jurors.  If you believe that the court interpreter

translated  testimony  incorrectly,  it  is  your  duty  to  immediately  notify

the bailiff or the court.
