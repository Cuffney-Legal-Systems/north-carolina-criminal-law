embalming]  [practice  of  funeral  directing]  [practice  of  funeral  services]

[operation of a funeral establishment].

For you to find the defendant guilty of this offense, the State must prove

three things beyond a reasonable doubt:

First, that the defendant was not duly [licensed] (and) [registered] to

[practice  embalming]  [practice  funeral  directing]  [practice  funeral  services]

[operate a funeral establishment] in this State;1

directing]  [practiced  funeral  services]  [operated  a  funeral  establishment]  in

this State;
