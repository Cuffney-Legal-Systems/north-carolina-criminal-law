or more of these things, it would be your duty to return a verdict of not guilty.

1. For the purposes of this offense, a delinquent juvenile in the custody of the Division
of  Juvenile  Justice  of  the  Department  of  Public  Safety  shall  mean  a  juvenile  confined  in  a
youth development center or a detention facility as defined in N.C. Gen. Stat. §7B-1501, and
shall include transportation of a juvenile to or from confinement.

2. A person acts “knowingly” when the person is aware or conscious of what he or she

is doing.
