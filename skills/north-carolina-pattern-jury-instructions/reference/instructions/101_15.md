GENERAL CRIMINAL VOLUME

------------------------------

101.15  CREDIBILITY OF WITNESS.

You are the sole judges of the believability of (a) witness(es).

You must decide for yourselves whether to believe the testimony of

any  witness.    You  may  believe  all,  any  part,  or  none  of  a  witness s

testimony.

In deciding whether to believe a witness you should use the same

tests of truthfulness that you use in your everyday lives.    Among  other

things, these tests may include: the opportunity of the  witness  to  see,

hear,  know,  or  remember  the  facts  or  occurrences  about  which  the

witness  testified;  the  manner  and  appearance  of  the  witness;  any

interest, bias, prejudice or partiality the witness may have; the apparent

understanding  and  fairness  of  the  witness;  whether  the  testimony  is

reasonable;  and  whether  the  testimony

is  consistent  with  other

believable evidence in the case.
