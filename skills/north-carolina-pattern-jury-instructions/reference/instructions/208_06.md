208.06  CASTRATION OR OTHER MAIMING WITHOUT MALICE
AFORETHOUGHT.  FELONY.

The defendant has been charged with [castration] [maiming].

For you to find the defendant guilty of this offense, the State must

prove two things beyond a reasonable doubt:

First,  that  the  defendant  [cut]  [slit]  [bit  off]  [cut  off]  [disabled]

[maimed] [disfigured] the victim's [nose] [lip] [ear] [(name limb)] [(name

member)] [(name privy member)], thereby permanently injuring him.

And Second, that the defendant did this on purpose and unlawfully

with  the  intent  to  [kill]  [maim]  [disfigure]  [disable]  [render  impotent]1

the victim.

NOTE WELL:  If self-defense is an issue, use N.C.P.I.-
Crim. 308.40 or 308.45, as appropriate.

If you find from the evidence beyond a reasonable doubt that on or

about the alleged  date,  the  defendant,  on  purpose  and  unlawfully  with

the  intent  to  [kill]  [maim]  [disfigure]  [disable]  [render  impotent]  the

victim [cut] [slit] [bit off] [cut off] [disabled]  [maimed]  [disfigured]  the

victim's (name injured part of body) thereby permanently injuring him, it

would be your duty to return a verdict of guilty.  If you do not so find or

have a reasonable doubt as to one or more of these things, it would be

your duty to return a verdict of not guilty.

NOTE WELL:  If self-defense is an issue, use mandate
from N.C.P.I.-Crim. 308.40 or 308.45, as appropriate.

1. If a definition of intent is required, see N.C.P.I.-Crim. 120.10.
