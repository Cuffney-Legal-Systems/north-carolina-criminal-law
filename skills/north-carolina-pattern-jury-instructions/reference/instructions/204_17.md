204.17. FACTORS THAT ENHANCE SENTENCE—WEARING [MASK] [DEVICE]
[CLOTHING] TO CONCEAL IDENTITY DURING THE COMMISSION OF THE
OFFENSE. MISDEMEANOR; FELONY.1

NOTE WELL:  This instruction should be used where a defendant
has  been  convicted  of  a  misdemeanor  or  felony  and  there  is
evidence  that  the  defendant  wore  a  mask,  device,  or  other
clothing to conceal or attempt to conceal the defendant’s identity
at the time of the offense.  This enhancement is not appropriate
and this instruction should not be used where the evidence that
the defendant wore a mask, device, or other clothing to conceal
or attempt to conceal the defendant’s identity is needed to prove
an  element  of  the  underlying  offense  charged.   See  N.C.  Gen.
Stat. § 15A-1340.16G(d).

See  N.C.  Gen.  Stat.  §  15A-928  for  provisions  regarding
indictment, bifurcated trial, verdict, and judgment.

In the event you have found the defendant guilty of (name misdemeanor

or felony), you must then consider and answer the following question:

Did  the  defendant  wear  [a  mask]  [a  device]  [clothing]  to  [conceal]

[attempt  to  conceal]  the  defendant’s  identity  at  the  time  the  defendant

committed (name misdemeanor or felony)?

On this question, the burden of proof is on the State to prove beyond a

reasonable doubt that the defendant wore [a mask] [a device] [clothing] to

[conceal]  [attempt  to  conceal]  the  defendant’s  identity  at  the  time  the

defendant committed (name misdemeanor or felony).

If  you  find  from  the  evidence  beyond  a  reasonable  doubt  that  the

defendant  wore  [a  mask]  [a  device]  [clothing]  to  [conceal]  [attempt  to

conceal] the defendant’s identity at the time the defendant committed (name

misdemeanor or felony), it would be your duty to answer this question "yes."

If you do not so find, or have a reasonable doubt, it would be your duty

to answer this question "no."2

1. N.C. Gen. Stat. § 15A-1340.16G(a) provides that a defendant who is convicted of a
misdemeanor or felony and who is found to have worn a mask, device, or other clothing to
conceal or attempt to conceal the defendant’s identity at the time of the offense is guilty of a
misdemeanor or felony that is one class higher than the underlying misdemeanor or felony
for which the person is convicted.

2. If this instruction is used, the following should be added to the verdict sheet:  "If
you have found the defendant guilty of (name misdemeanor or felony), do you find that the
defendant  wore  [a  mask]  [a  device]  [clothing]  to  [conceal]  [attempt  to  conceal]  the
defendant’s  identity  at  the  time  the  defendant  committed  (name  misdemeanor  or
felony)?  Answer: ____________________."
