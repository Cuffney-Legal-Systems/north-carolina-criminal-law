271.44 [DISPLAYING] [CAUSING TO BE DISPLAYED] [PERMITTING TO BE
DISPLAYED] [POSSESSING] A [REGISTRATION CARD] [CERTIFICATE OF
TITLE] [REGISTRATION NUMBER PLATE] THAT IS [FICTITIOUS] [HAS BEEN
[CANCELLED] [REVOKED] [SUSPENDED] [ALTERED]]. INFRACTION.

The  defendant  has  been  charged  with  [displaying]  [causing  to  be

displayed]  [permitting  to  be  displayed]  [possessing]  a  [registration  card]

[certificate of title] [registration number plate] for a vehicle knowing that the

[[registration  card]  [certificate  of  title]  [registration  number  plate]]  [[was

fictitious] [had been [canceled] [revoked] [suspended] [altered]]].

For  you  to  find  the  defendant  responsible  of  this  offense,  the  State

must prove two things beyond a reasonable doubt:

First,  that  the  defendant  [displayed]  [caused  to  be  displayed]

[permitted  to  be  displayed]  [possessed]  a  [registration  card]  [certificate  of

title] [registration number plate] on a vehicle.

And  Second,  that  the  defendant  knew  that  the  [[registration  card]

[certificate  of  title]  [registration  number  plate]]  [[was  fictitious]  [had  been

[canceled] [revoked] [suspended] [altered]]].

If  you  find  from  the  evidence  beyond  a  reasonable  doubt  that  on  or

about  the  alleged  date,  the  defendant  [displayed]  [caused  to  be  displayed]

[permitted  to  be  displayed]  [possessed]  a  [registration  card]  [certificate  of

title] [registration number plate] for a vehicle and that the defendant knew

the  [[registration  card]  [certificate  of  title]  [registration  number  plate]]

[[was  fictitious]  [had  been  [canceled]  [revoked]  [suspended]  [altered]]],  it

would be your duty to return a verdict of responsible.  If you do not so find

or  have  a  reasonable  doubt  as  to  one  or  more  of  these  things,  it  would  be

your duty to return a verdict of not responsible.
