[present] [[file the [return] [affidavit] [claim] [(describe other document)]];

1  To act willfully in this context is “a voluntary, intentional violation of a known legal

duty.” See Cheek v. United States, 498 U.S. 192, 200, 111 S. Ct. 604, 610, 112 L. Ed. 2d

2 N.C. Gen. Stat. § 105-228.90(b)(4) defines an income tax return preparer as “any
person who prepares for compensation, or who employs one or more person to prepare for
compensation, any return of tax imposed by or any claim for refund of tax imposed by
Article 4 of this Chapter.” For purposes of this definition, the completion of a substantial
portion of a return or claim for refund is treated as the preparation of the return or claim for
refund.
