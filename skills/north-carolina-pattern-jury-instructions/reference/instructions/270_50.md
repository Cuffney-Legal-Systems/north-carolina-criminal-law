270.50 SPEEDING IN EXCESS OF [15 MPH MORE THAN SPEED LIMIT] [80
MPH]. MISDEMEANOR.1

The defendant has been charged with speeding in excess of [15 miles

per hour more than the established speed limit] [80 miles per hour].

For  you  to  find  the  defendant  guilty  of  this  offense,  the  State  must

prove four things beyond a reasonable doubt:

First,  that  the  defendant  drove  a  vehicle.  (A  (describe  vehicle)  is  a

vehicle.)

Second, that the defendant drove on a highway. (Describe place) is a

highway.

Third, that the legal speed limit where the defendant drove was (state

speed limit).

And Fourth, that the defendant drove the vehicle at a speed in excess

of  (state  speed  more  than  15  miles  per  hour  over  the  speed  limit)  (state

speed more than 80 miles per hour).

If  you  find  from  the  evidence  beyond  a  reasonable  doubt  that  on  or

about the alleged date the defendant drove a vehicle on a highway and that

the  legal  speed  limit  at  this  place  was  (state  limit)  and  that  the  defendant

drove the vehicle at a speed in excess of [15 miles per hour more than the

estimated speed limit] [80 miles per hour], it would be your duty to return a

verdict of guilty. If you do not so find or have a reasonable doubt as to one

or  more  of  these  things,  you  would  not  return  a  verdict  of  guilty  of  this

offense  but  must  consider  whether  the  defendant  is  responsible  for

exceeding the legal speed limit.
