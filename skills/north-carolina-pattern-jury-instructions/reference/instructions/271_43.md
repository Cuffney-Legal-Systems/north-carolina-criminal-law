271.43  WILLFULLY DISPLAYING AN EXPIRED [LICENSE] [REGISTRATION
PLATE] ON A VEHICLE KNOWING THE SAME TO BE EXPIRED.
INFRACTION.

The defendant has been charged with willfully displaying an expired

[license]  [registration  plate]  on  a  vehicle  knowing  the  same  to  be

expired.

For you to find the defendant responsible of this offense, the State

must prove two things beyond a reasonable doubt:

First,  that  the  defendant  willfully  displayed  an  expired  [license]

[registration plate] on a vehicle.

And  Second,

that

the  defendant  knew

that

the  [license]

[registration plate] was expired.

If you find from the evidence beyond a reasonable doubt that on or

about  the  alleged  date,  the  defendant  willfully  displayed  an  expired

[license] [registration  plate]  on  a  vehicle  and  that  the  defendant  knew

that the [license] [registration plate] was expired, it would be your duty

to  return  a  verdict  of  responsible.    If  you  do  not  so  find  or  have  a

reasonable doubt as to one or more of these things, it would be your duty

to return a verdict of not responsible.
