104.94  TESTIMONY OF EXPERT WITNESS.

In this case you have heard evidence from [a witness] [witnesses]

who [has] [have] testified as (an) expert witness(es).  An expert witness

is  permitted  to  testify  in  the  form  of  an  opinion  in  a  field  where  the

witness purports to have specialized skill or knowledge.

As I have instructed you, you are the sole judges of the credibility

of  each  witness  and  the  weight  to  be  given  to  the  testimony  of  each

witness.  In making this determination as to the testimony of an expert

witness, you should consider, in addition to the other tests of credibility

and weight, the witness's training, qualifications, and experience or lack

thereof; the reasons, if any, given for the opinion; whether the opinion is

supported by facts that you find from the evidence; whether the opinion

is reasonable; and whether it is consistent with other believable evidence

in the case.

You should consider the opinion of an expert witness, but you are

not bound by it.  In other words, you are not required to accept an expert

witness's  opinion  to  the  exclusion  of  the  facts  and  circumstances

disclosed by other testimony.
