------------------------------

105.10 RELIGIOUS BELIEFS OR OPINIONS.

When  evidence  has  been  received  with  regard  to  a  witness'

religious beliefs or opinions, you must not consider that by reason of such

evidence  that  witness'  credibility  is  impaired  or  enhanced.    You  may,

however,  consider  such  evidence  for  the  purpose  of  showing  [interest]

[bias]  and  therefore  what  weight  you  wish  to  accord  that  witness'

testimony.
