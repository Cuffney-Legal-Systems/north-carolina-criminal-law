259.80  MISUSE OF 911 SYSTEM. MISDEMEANOR.

The defendant has been charged with misuse of the 911 system.

For you to find the defendant guilty of this offense, the State must

prove two things beyond a reasonable doubt.

First, that the defendant was not [seeking public safety assistance]

[providing 911 service] [responding to a 911 call].

Second,  that  the  defendant  knowingly  [accessed]  [attempted  to

access]  the  911  system  for  a  purpose  other  than  an  emergency

communication.

If you find from the evidence beyond a reasonable doubt that on or

about the alleged date the defendant knowingly [accessed] [attempted to

access]  the  911  system  for  a  purpose  other  than  an  emergency

communication and was not [seeking public safety assistance] [providing

911 service] [responding to a 911 call], it would be your duty to return a

verdict of guilty. If you do not so find or have a reasonable doubt as to

one or more of these things, it would be your duty to return a verdict of

not guilty.
