------------------------------

104.95  OPINION TESTIMONY BY LAY WITNESS.

Evidence  has  been  received  from  a  witness  in  the  form  of  an

opinion. You may only consider the opinion of a witness that is rationally

based  on  the  perception  of  the  witness  and  helpful  to  [a  clear

understanding  of  the  witness's  testimony]  (or)  [the  determination  of  a

fact in issue].
