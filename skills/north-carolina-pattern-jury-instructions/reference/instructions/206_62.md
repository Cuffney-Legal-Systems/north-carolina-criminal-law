206.62  MURDER OF UNBORN CHILD - [MURDER] [VOLUNTARY
MANSLAUGHTER] [INVOLUNTARY MANSLAUGHTER] OF MOTHER. FELONY.

NOTE  WELL:  The  statutes  provide  that  a  person  who
causes  the  death  of  an  unborn  child  by  an  act  that
would  amount  to  the  (attempted)  [murder]  [voluntary
manslaughter] [involuntary manslaughter]1 if it resulted
in the death of the mother would also be guilty of the
separate offense of [murder]  [voluntary  manslaughter]
[involuntary manslaughter] of an unborn child.

To instruct for this offense, the appropriate  murder  or
manslaughter charge would be used in  relation  to  the
mother,  i.e.,  the  victim  in  the  underlying  murder  or
manslaughter  charge  would  be  the  mother.      This
instruction provides language that may be inserted into
the  various  murder  and  manslaughter  instructions  to
charge for the separate offense involving the death of
an unborn child.  Other modifications to the applicable
murder or manslaughter instructions may be necessary
depending upon the circumstances.

Use as introduction to charge:

The  defendant  has  been  charged  with  causing  the  death  of  an

unborn  child2  by  an  act  that  would  be  (attempted)  [murder]  [voluntary

manslaughter]  [involuntary  manslaughter]  if  it  resulted  in  the  death  of

the mother.

Add  this  additional  element  to  the  body  of  the

instruction and mandate:

That the defendant!s act of (describe act) caused the death of an

unborn child.

perpetration or attempted perpetration of any of the criminal offenses set forth under N.C.
