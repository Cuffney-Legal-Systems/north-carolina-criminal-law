The defendant is charged with assault on a law enforcement officer

while the officer was performing or attempting to perform a duty of  the

officer!s office.

prove four things beyond a reasonable doubt.

First, that the defendant assaulted (name officer) by  intentionally

(describe assault, e.g., striking him).

was a law enforcement officer. A (state alleged victim s position, e.g., a
