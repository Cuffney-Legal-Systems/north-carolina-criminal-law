238.23A  PROMOTING PROSTITUTION OF A MINOR (SUPERVISING
PROSTITUTION).  CLASS D FELONY.

NOTE  WELL:  This  statute  has  been  repealed  by  Session  Laws
2013-368,  s.4,  effective  October  1,  2013.  This  instruction
applies only to offenses committed prior to October 1, 2013.

NOTE  WELL:    For  offenses  occurring  on  or  after  December  1,
1995 this offense is a Class D felony.

N.C. Gen. Stat. § 14-190.18(b) provides that a mistake of

age is not a defense to prosecution.

minor.2

prove three things beyond a reasonable doubt:

First,

that

Second, and that person was a minor.

1 N.C. Gen. Stat. § 14-203(5) defines “prostitution” as “the [performance of] [offer
