MAY 2005
------------------------------

104.60  ADMISSIONS.

If you  find  from  the  evidence  that  the  defendant  has  admitted  a

fact relating to the crime charged in this case, then you should consider

all of the circumstances under which it was made in determining whether

it was a truthful admission and the weight you will give to it.
