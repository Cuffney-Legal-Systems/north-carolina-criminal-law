REMARKS TO JURORS BEFORE CHARGE CONFERENCE.
GENERAL CRIMINAL VOLUME
JUNE 2015
------------------------------

100.38  REMARKS TO JURORS BEFORE CHARGE CONFERENCE.

NOTE WELL: This is a suggestive model and the judge
can modify as deemed appropriate

Members of the jury, all of the evidence has now been presented. It

will soon be your duty to decide from the evidence what the facts are and

to apply the law that I am about to give you to those facts in arriving at

your verdict in this case.

Prior to the arguments of the lawyers and the final instructions of

the court on the law that will follow the lawyers' speeches, I am required

to confer with the lawyers about the law involved in  this  case.  For  this

purpose, you will now be excused to the jury room while we conduct our

charge conference, after which we will proceed with the conclusion of this

case. Do not yet allow your minds to be made  up  in  this  case  because

you have not yet heard the arguments of the  lawyers  or  instructions  of

the court. Again, I caution you not to discuss the case with each other or

anyone else or permit anyone to discuss it in your presence or speak to

anyone involved in the case in this period.
