101.32  DEFENDANT'S ABSENCE FROM TRIAL; CLOSED CIRCUIT
TELEVISION MONITORING.

In compliance with court procedure that is provided for in cases of

this  nature,  the  defendant  will  not  be  present  during  portions  of  the

presentation  of  evidence.  In  his  absence,  the  defendant  is  provided

closed circuit television monitoring of the proceedings and will be allowed

to confer with his attorney during the presentation of evidence.

The defendant's absence is not to create any presumption against

him and is not to influence your decision in any way.
