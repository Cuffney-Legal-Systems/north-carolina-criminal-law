235.30  POINTING A LASER DEVICE TOWARDS AN AIRCRAFT.  FELONY.

The defendant has been charged with pointing a laser device at an

aircraft.

For you to find the defendant guilty of this offense, the State must

prove two things beyond a reasonable doubt:

First, the defendant willfully pointed a laser1 device at an aircraft2,

while the laser device was emitting a laser beam.

And  Second,  the  laser  beam  was  emitted  while  the  aircraft  was

[taking off] [landing] [in flight] [in motion].

If you find from the evidence beyond a reasonable doubt that on or

about the alleged date, the defendant willfully pointed a laser device at

an  aircraft,  while  the  device  was  emitting  a  laser  beam  and  while  the

aircraft was [taking off] [landing] [in flight] [in motion], it would be your

duty  to  return  a  verdict  of  guilty.    If  you  do  not  so  find  or  have  a

reasonable doubt as to one or both of these things, it would be your duty

to return a verdict of not guilty.

1. !Aircraft" is defined in N.C. Gen. Stat.   63-1.

2. !Laser" is defined in N.C. Gen. Stat.   14-34.8.
