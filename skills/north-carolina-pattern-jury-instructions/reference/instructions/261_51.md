MISDEMEANOR.

NOTE WELL: For a  first  offense,  the  retailer  would  be
guilty  of  a  misdemeanor.  For  a  second  or  subsequent
offense, the retailer would be guilty of a felony.

indictment,  bifurcated

The defendant has been charged with failing to lawfully purchase a

pseudoephedrine product.

For you to find the defendant guilty of this offense, the state must

prove two (three) things beyond a reasonable doubt:

First, that the defendant was a purchaser1;

Second, that the defendant willfully and knowingly

a)  [[purchased]  [attempted  to  purchase]  at  retail  more  than  3.6

grams of any  pseudoephedrine  products  in  a  single  calendar

day]

b)  [[purchased]  [attempted  to  purchase]  at  retail  more  than  9

grams of pseudoephedrine products within any 30-day period];

(And  Third,  that  on  (name  date)  the  defendant  in  (name  court)

[was convicted of] [pled guilty to] the crime of (describe pseudoephedrine

sales crime under N.C. Gen. Stat.   90-113.56), that was committed on

(name  date)  in  violation  of  the  laws  of  the  [State  of  North  Carolina]

[State of (name other state)] [United States].)
