IDENTIFICATION OF A DEFENDANT AS PERPETRATOR OF A CRIME.
GENERAL CRIMINAL VOLUME
APRIL 2005
------------------------------

104.90  IDENTIFICATION OF A DEFENDANT AS PERPETRATOR OF A CRIME.

I instruct you that the State has the burden of proving the identity

of  the  defendant  as  the  perpetrator  of  the  crime  charged  beyond  a

reasonable  doubt.    This  means  that  you,  the  jury,  must  be  satisfied

beyond a reasonable doubt that the defendant was the perpetrator of the

crime charged before you may return a verdict of guilty.
