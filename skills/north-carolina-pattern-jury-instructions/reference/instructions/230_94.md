230.94  DISTURBING HUMAN REMAINS - PHYSICAL ALTERATION OR
MANIPULATION. FELONY.

The  defendant  has  been  charged  with  [disturbing]  [vandalizing]

[desecrating] human remains1 by [physical alteration] [manipulation].

For you to find the defendant guilty of this offense, the State must

prove two things beyond a reasonable doubt:

First,  that  the  defendant  [disturbed]  [vandalized]  [desecrated]

human  remains  by  [physical  alteration]  [manipulation]  [(describe  other

means)] of the human remains;

And Second, that the defendant acted willfully.

If you find from the evidence beyond a reasonable doubt that on or

about  the  alleged  date  the  defendant  willfully  [disturbed]  [vandalized]

[desecrated]  human  remains  by  [physical  alteration]  [manipulation]

[(describe other means)] of the human remains, it would be your duty to

return  a  verdict  of  guilty.    If  you  do  not  so  find  or  have  a  reasonable

doubt as to one or more of these things, then it would be your duty to

return a verdict of not guilty.

1. N.C. Gen. Stat.   14-401.22 (f) defines !human remains" as !any dead human body
in any condition of decay or any significant part of a dead human body, including any limb,
organ, or bone."
