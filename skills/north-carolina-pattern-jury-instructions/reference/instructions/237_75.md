237.75  OPERATING ELECTRONIC SWEEPSTAKES. MISDEMEANOR,
FELONY.

The  defendant  has  been  charged  with  [operating]  [placing  into

operation]  an  electronic  [machine]  [device]

for

the  purpose  of

[conducting]  [promoting]  a  sweepstakes

through  the  use  of  an

entertaining  display,  including  the  entry  process  or  the  revealing  of  a

prize.

For you to find the defendant guilty of this offense the State must

prove two things beyond a reasonable doubt:

First,  that  the  defendant  [operated]  [placed  into  operation]  an

electronic [machine] [device].1

And  Second,  that  the  defendant  did  so  for  the  purpose  of

[conducting]  [promoting]  a  sweepstakes2  through  the  use  of  an

entertaining display3, including  the  entry4  process  or  the  revealing  of  a

prize5.

If you find from the evidence beyond a reasonable doubt that on or

about the alleged date the defendant [operated] [placed into operation]

an  electronic  [machine]  [device]

for  the  purpose  of  [conducting]

[promoting]  a  sweepstakes  through  the  use  of  an  entertaining  display,

including the entry process or the revealing of a prize, it would be  your

duty to return a verdict of guilty. If you do not so find or if you have a

reasonable doubt as to one or more of these things, it would be your duty

to return a verdict of not guilty.

2. "Sweepstakes" means any game, advertising scheme or plan, or other promotion,
which, with or without payment of any consideration, a person may enter to win or become
eligible to receive any prize, the determination of which is based upon chance. N.C. Gen.
Stat.   14-306.4(a)(5)

3.  "Entertaining  display"  means  visual  information,  capable  of  being  seen  by  a
sweepstakes entrant, that takes the form of actual  game  play,  or  simulated  game  play,
such as, by way of illustration and not exclusion:

a) A video poker game or any other kind of video playing card game.
b) A video bingo game.
c) A video craps game.
d) A video keno game.
e) A video lotto game.
f) Eight liner.
g) Pot-of-gold.
h) A video game based on or involving the random or chance matching of different
pictures, words, numbers, or symbols not dependent on the skill or dexterity of
the player.

4. "Enter" or "entry" means the act or process by which a person becomes eligible to

receive any prize offered in a sweepstakes. N.C. Gen. Stat.   14-306.4(a)(2)

5. "Prize" means any gift, award, gratuity, good, service, credit, or anything else of
value, which may be transferred to a person, whether possession of the prize is actually
transferred, or placed on an account or other record as evidence of the intent to transfer
the prize. N.C. Gen. Stat.   14-306.4(a)(4)
