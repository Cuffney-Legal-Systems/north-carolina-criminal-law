1. N.C. Gen. Stat.   14-458.2 (a)(2) defines !student" as !a person who has been
assigned to a school by a local board of education as provided in N.C. Gen. Stat.   115C-
366; or has enrolled in a charter school authorized under N.C. Gen. Stat.   115C-238.29D, a
regional school created under N.C. Gen. Stat.   115C-238.62, or a nonpublic school which
has filed intent to operate under Part 1 or  Part  2  of  Article  39  of  Chapter  115C  of  the
General  Statutes,  or  a  person  who  has  been  suspended  or  expelled  from  any  of  these
schools within the last year."

2. N.C. Gen. Stat.   14-458.2 (a)(1) defines !school employee" as !(a) An employee
of a local board of education, a charter school authorized under N.C. Gen. Stat.   115C-
238.29D, a regional school created under N.C. Gen. Stat.   115C-238.62, or  a  nonpublic
school which has filed intent to operate under Part 1 or Part 2 of Article 39 of Chapter 115C
of the General statutes. (b) An independent contractor or an employee of an independent
contractor of a local board of education, a charter school authorized under N.C. Gen. Stat.
   115C-238.29D,  a  regional  school  created  under  N.C.  Gen.  Stat.     115C-238.62,  or  a
nonpublic school which has filed intent to operate under Part 1 or Part 2 of Article 39 of
Chapter 115C  of  the  General  Statutes,  if  the  independent  contractor  carries  out  duties
customarily performed by employees of the school."
