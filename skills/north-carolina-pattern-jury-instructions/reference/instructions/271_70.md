271.70  LEAVING SCENE AFTER ACCIDENT RESULTING IN [INJURY]
[DEATH] TO ANY PERSON - PASSENGER.  FELONY.

The  defendant  has  been  charged  with  leaving  the  scene  of  an

[accident] [collision] resulting in [injury] [death] to any person.

For you to find the defendant guilty of this offense, the State must

prove four things beyond a reasonable doubt:

First, that the defendant was the passenger of a vehicle that was

involved  in  an  [accident]  [collision]  resulting  in  [injury]  [death]  to  any

person.

Second, that the defendant knew or reasonably should have known

that  the  vehicle  was  involved  in  an  [accident]  [collision]  resulting  in

[injury] [death] to any person.

Third,  that  defendant  willfully  left  the  scene  of  the  accident  by

acting  as  the  driver  of  a  vehicle  involved  in  the  accident  (without

justification or excuse).1

And Fourth, that a law enforcement officer had not [completed the

investigation  of  the  accident]  [authorized  the  defendant  to  leave  the

scene of the accident].

If you find from the evidence beyond a reasonable doubt that on or

about the alleged date the defendant was the passenger of a vehicle that

was  involved  in  an  [accident]  [collision],  that  the  defendant  knew  or

reasonably  should  have  known  that  the  vehicle  was  involved  in  the

[accident] [collision] resulting in [injury] [death] to any person, and that

the  defendant  willfully  left  the  scene  of  the  accident  before  a  law
