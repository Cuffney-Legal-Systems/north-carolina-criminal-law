271.23  SEX OFFENDER DRIVING [COMMERCIAL PASSENGER VEHICLE]
[SCHOOL BUS].  FELONY.

NOTE  WELL:  This  instruction  applies  to  defendants
whose initial registration under Article  27A  of  Chapter
14 of the General Statutes occurs on or after December
1, 2009, and to defendants who were registered under
Article 27A of Chapter 14 of the General Statutes prior
to December 1, 2009, and continue to be registered on
or after December 1, 2009.

The  defendant  has  been  charged  with  driving  a  [commercial

passenger vehicle] [school bus] without  a  license,  where  the  defendant

did not have  a  license  because  the  defendant  had  been  convicted  of  a

violation that  requires  registration  as  a  sex  offender  pursuant  to  North

Carolina law.1

For you to find the defendant guilty of this offense, the State must

prove two things beyond a reasonable doubt:

First,  that  the  defendant  drove  a  [commercial  passenger  vehicle]

[school bus].

And  Second,  that  the  defendant  did  not  have  a  valid  commercial

drivers  license  with  a  [P  endorsement]2  [S  endorsement]3  because  the

defendant had been convicted of a violation that requires registration as

a sex offender pursuant to North Carolina law.

If you find from the evidence beyond a reasonable doubt that on or

about  the  alleged  date,  the  defendant  drove  a  [commercial  passenger

vehicle]  [school  bus]  and  that  the  defendant  did  not  have  a  valid

commercial  drivers  license  with  a  [P  endorsement]  [S  endorsement]

because  the  defendant  had  been  convicted  of  a  violation  that  requires

registration as a sex offender pursuant to North Carolina law, it would be
