The  defendant  has  been  charged  with  participating  in  dog  fighting  or

baiting.

For  you  to  find  the  defendant  guilty  of  this  offense,  the  State  must

prove two things beyond a reasonable doubt.

First, that the defendant

a)  [instigated] [promoted] [conducted] [was employed at] [provided a

dog  for]  [allowed  property  under  his  ownership  or  control  to  be

used  for]  [gambled  on]  [profited  from]  an  exhibition  featuring  the

[fighting] [baiting] of a dog.] (or)

b)  [owned]  [possessed]  [trained]  a  dog  to  be  used  in  an  exhibition

featuring the [fighting] [baiting] of that dog.] (or)

c)  [participated as a spectator at an exhibition featuring the [fighting]

[baiting] of a dog.]

And Second, that the defendant did this intentionally,1 that is without

justification or excuse.
