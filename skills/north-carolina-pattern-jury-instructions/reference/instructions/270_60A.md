270.60A  UNSAFE MOVEMENT CAUSING [PROPERTY DAMAGE] [PERSONAL
INJURY] TO MOTORCYCLE OPERATOR. INFRACTION.

The  defendant  has  been  charged  with  causing  [property  damage]

[personal injury] to a motorcycle [operator] [passenger].

For  you  to  find  the  defendant  responsible  for  this  infraction,  the

State must prove seven things beyond a reasonable doubt:

First, that the defendant was the driver of a vehicle;

Second, that the vehicle was on a [highway] [public vehicular area];

Third, that defendant [started] [stopped] [turned the vehicle from a

direct line];

Fourth, that the [start] [stop] [turn] could not be made safely; It is

not required that the circumstances be absolutely free from  danger.    In

determining whether the driver can [start] [stop] [turn] with safety, the

driver has the right to assume, in the absence of circumstances indicating

the  contrary,  that  other  drivers  will  exercise  reasonable  care  to  avoid

colliding with the driver!s vehicle.

Fifth, that before [starting] [stopping] [turning] the defendant  did

not see that the movement could be made in safety;

Sixth, that when the defendant [started]  [stopped]  [turned]  there

was  a  motorcycle  operator  who  was  affected  by  this  movement  and

defendant caused this motorcycle operator to [change travel lanes] [leave

that portion of any [public street] [highway] designated as travel lanes];

And  Seventh,  that  the  defendant!s  actions  resulted  in  a  crash

causing  [property  damage]  [personal  injury]  to  a  motorcycle  [operator]

[passenger].
