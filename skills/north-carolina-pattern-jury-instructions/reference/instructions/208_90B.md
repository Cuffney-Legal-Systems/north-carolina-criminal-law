208.90B [DISCHARGING] [ATTEMPTING TO DISCHARGE] A FIREARM
WITHIN AN OCCUPIED BUILDING OR OTHER ENCLOSURE WITH INTENT
TO INCITE FEAR. FELONY.1

The defendant has been charged with [discharging] [attempting to

discharge] a firearm within an occupied building or  other  enclosure  with

the intent to incite fear.

For you to find the defendant guilty of this offense, the State must

prove two things beyond a reasonable doubt:

First,  that  the  defendant  [[willfully]  [wantonly]]  [discharged]

[attempted  to  discharge]  a  firearm  within  an  occupied  [building]

[structure]  [motor  vehicle]  [(describe  other  conveyance)]  [erection]

[enclosure].

Second,  that  the  defendant  intended  to  incite  fear  in  another

person.

If you find from the evidence beyond a reasonable doubt that on or

about

the  alleged  date,

the  defendant

[[willfully]

[wantonly]]

[discharged]  [attempted  to  discharge]  a  firearm  within  an  occupied

[building]  [structure]  [motor  vehicle]  [(describe  other  conveyance)]

[erection] [enclosure] with the intent to incite fear in another, it would be

your duty to return a  verdict  of  guilty.  If  you  do  not  so  find  or  have  a

reasonable doubt as to one or more of these things, it would be your duty

to return a verdict of not guilty.

1 Unless covered under some other provision of law providing greater punishment, this

crime is punishable as a Class F felony.
