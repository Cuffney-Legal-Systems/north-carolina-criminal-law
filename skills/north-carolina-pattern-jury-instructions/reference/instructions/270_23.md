The defendant has been charged with operating a [school bus] [school

activity bus] [child care vehicle] [ambulance] [emergency medical services

vehicle, that is an EMS vehicle] [firefighting vehicle] [law enforcement

vehicle] after consuming alcohol.1

For you to find the defendant guilty of this offense, the State must

prove thee things beyond a reasonable doubt.

First, that the defendant operated a [school bus] [school activity bus]

[child care vehicle] [ambulance] [EMS vehicle] [firefighting vehicle] [law

enforcement vehicle].

Second, that the defendant operated said vehicle after consuming

alcohol.

Third, that the defendant drove the vehicle on a [highway] [street]

[public vehicular area] within this state while the alcohol remained in the

defendant’s body.

If you find from the evidence beyond a reasonable doubt that on or

about the alleged date the defendant operated a [school bus] [school activity

bus] [child care vehicle] [ambulance] [EMS vehicle] [firefighting vehicle]

[law enforcement vehicle] within this state on a [highway] [street] [public

vehicular area] after consuming alcohol while the alcohol remained in the
