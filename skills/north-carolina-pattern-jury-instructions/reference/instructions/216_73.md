216.73  FELONIOUS [PURCHASING] [DISPOSING OF] [SELLING]
[TRANSFERRING] [RECEIVING] [POSSESSING] A [MOTOR VEHICLE]
[MOTOR VEHICLE PART] FROM A PERSON ENGAGED IN CHOP SHOP
ACTIVITY.1 FELONY.

NOTE WELL. N.C. Gen. Stat. § 14-72.7(b) provides for innocent
activities to which the prohibition of this section does not apply.

The  defendant  has  been  charged  with

felonious  [purchasing]

[disposing  of]  [selling]  [transferring]  [receiving]  [possessing]  a  [motor

vehicle] [motor vehicle part] from a person engaged in (describe chop shop

activity).

For  you  to  find  the  defendant  guilty  of  this  offense,  the  State  must

prove two things beyond a reasonable doubt:

First, that the defendant [purchased] [disposed of] [sold] [transferred]

[received] [possessed] a [motor vehicle] [motor vehicle part] from a person

engaged in (describe chop shop activity).

And  Second,  that  the  defendant  [knew]  [had  reasonable  grounds  to

believe] the person was engaged in (describe chop shop activity).

If  you  find  from  the  evidence  beyond  a  reasonable  doubt  that  on  or

about  the  alleged  date,  the  defendant  knowingly  [purchased]  [disposed  of]

[sold] [transferred] [received] [possessed] a [motor vehicle] [motor vehicle

part]  from  a  person  engaged  in  (describe  chop  shop  activity)  and  that  the

defendant  [knew]  [had  reasonable  grounds  to  believe]  the  person  was

engaged  in  (describe  chop  shop  activity),  it  would  be  your  duty  to  return  a

verdict of guilty. If you do not so find or have a reasonable doubt as to one

or  both  of  these  things,  it  would  be  your  duty  to  return  a  verdict  of  not
