120.12  DEFINITION OF SERIOUS INJURY.

NOTE WELL:  For the definition of  serious bodily injury!
use N.C.P.I.-Crim. 120.11.

Serious injury is injury that causes great pain and suffering.1

1. For mental injury constituting serious injury see S. v. Boone, 307 N.C. 198 (1982).
