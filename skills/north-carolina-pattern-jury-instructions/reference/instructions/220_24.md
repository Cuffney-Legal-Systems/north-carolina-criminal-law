220.24  IMPROPER FILING OF LIEN ON [REAL PROPERTY] [OTHER
DOCUMENT]. FELONY.

The  defendant  has  been  charged  with  improperly  filing  a  lien  on

real property.

For you to find the defendant guilty of this offense the State must

prove two things beyond a reasonable doubt:

First, that defendant [caused] [attempted to cause] a claim of lien

on real property1 to be filed;

And  Second,  that  the  defendant  did  so  [with  knowledge  that  the

filing was not authorized by statute] [[with the intent that the filing be

made  for  an  improper  purpose  such  as  to  [hinder]  [harass]  [wrongfully

interfere with] another person.

If you find from the evidence beyond a reasonable doubt that on or

about the alleged  date  the  defendant  [caused]  [attempted  to  cause]  a

claim of lien on real property to be filed and that the  defendant  did  so

[with knowledge that the filing was not authorized by statute] [[with the

intent that the filing be made for an improper purpose such as to [hinder]

[harass] [wrongfully interfere with] another person, it would be your duty

to return a verdict of guilty. If you do not so find or have a  reasonable

doubt as to one or more of these things, then it would be your duty to

return a verdict of not guilty.

1.  A  defendant  can  also  [cause]  [attempt  to  cause]  other  documents  to  be

improperly filed according to N.C. Gen. Stat.   44A-12.1 (c).
