If the defendant assaulted the victim in lawful defense of another

person,  the  defendant's  actions  would  be  excused,  and  the  defendant

evidence beyond a reasonable doubt that the defendant did not act in the

lawful defense of another person.

defendant  assaulted  the  victim  and  that  the  circumstances  would  have
