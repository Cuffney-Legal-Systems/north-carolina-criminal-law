FUNCTION OF THE JURY.
GENERAL CRIMINAL VOLUME
JUNE 2011
------------------------------

101.05  FUNCTION OF THE JURY.

Members of the jury:  All of the evidence has been presented.  It is

now your duty to decide from this evidence what the facts are.  You must

then apply the law which I am about to  give  you  to  those  facts.    It  is

absolutely necessary that you understand and apply the law as I give it

to you, and not as you think it is, or as you might like it to be.  This is

important because justice requires that everyone tried for the same crime

be treated in the same way and have the same law applied.
