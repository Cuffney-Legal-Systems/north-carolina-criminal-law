272.26  CONSUMPTION OF ALCOHOL BY A PERSON GREATER THAN 19
YEARS OF AGE BUT LESS THAN 21 YEARS OF AGE.1 MISDEMEANOR.

The defendant has been charged with consumption of alcohol by a

person greater than 19 years of age but less than 21 years of age.

For you to find the defendant guilty of this offense2, the State must

prove two things beyond a reasonable doubt:

First,  that  the  defendant  knowingly  consumed  (name  beverage).

(Name beverage) is an alcoholic beverage.

And Second, that at the time the defendant consumed the alcoholic

beverage, the defendant was greater than 19 years of age but less than

21 years of age.

If you find from the evidence beyond a reasonable doubt that on or

about the alleged  date  the  defendant  knowingly  consumed  an  alcoholic

beverage and that the defendant was greater than 19  years  of  age  but

less than 21 years of age,  it  would  be  your  duty  to  return  a  verdict  of

guilty.  If you do not  so  find  or  have  a  reasonable  doubt  as  to  one  or

more  of  these  things,  it  would  be  your  duty  to  return  a  verdict  of  not

guilty.

1 It shall not be unlawful for a person less than 21 years old to consume unfortified
wine or fortified wine during participation in an exempted activity under N.C. Gen. Stat.
18B-103(4), (8), or (11).

2 A person under the age of 21 shall not be prosecuted for a violation of N.C. Gen.
Stat.   18B-302 for  the  possession  of  alcoholic  beverages  if  law  enforcement,  including
campus  safety  police,  became  aware  of  the  possession  of  alcohol  by  the  person  solely
because  the  person  was  seeking  medical  assistance  for  another  individual.  This  limited
immunity shall apply if, when seeking medical assistance on behalf of another, the person
did all of the following: (1) acted in good faith, upon a reasonable belief that he or she was
the first to call for assistance; (2) used his or her own name when contacting authorities;
and (3) remained with the individual needing medical assistance until help arrived. N.C. Gen.
Stat.   18B-302.
