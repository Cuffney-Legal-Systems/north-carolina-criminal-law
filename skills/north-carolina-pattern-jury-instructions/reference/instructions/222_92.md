222.92. INJURING A PUBLIC WATER SYSTEM. FELONY.

The defendant has been charged with injuring a public water system.

For you to find the defendant guilty of injuring a public water system,

the State must prove three things beyond a reasonable doubt.

First, that the defendant [[stopped] [obstructed] [impaired] [weakened]

[destroyed] [injured] [otherwise damaged]] [attempted to [stop] [obstruct]

[impair]  [weaken]  [destroy]  [injure]  [otherwise  damage]]  the  [property]

[equipment] of a public water system.1

Second, that the defendant did so with the intent to impair the services

of the public water system.

And  Third,  that  the  defendant  acted  knowingly  and  willfully.  Willfully

means intentionally and without justification or excuse.

If  you  find  from  the  evidence  beyond  a  reasonable  doubt  that  on  or

about  the  alleged  date  the  defendant  knowingly  and  willfully  [[stopped]

[obstructed]  [impaired]  [weakened]  [destroyed]  [injured]  [otherwise

damaged]]  [attempted  to  [stop]  [obstruct]  [impair]  [weaken]  [destroy]

[injure]  [otherwise  damage]]  the  [property]  [equipment]  of  a  public  water

system with  the  intent to  impair the  services of the public water system, it

would be your duty to return a verdict of guilty. If you do not so find or have

reasonable doubt as to one or more of these things, it would be your duty to

return a verdict of not guilty.

1. For a definition of public water system, see N.C. Gen. Stat. § 130A-313(10).
