GENERAL CRIMINAL VOLUME
JUNE 2008
------------------------------

101.41  MULTIPLE DEFENDANTS1 - ONE DEFENDANT PLEADS GUILTY
DURING TRIAL.

NOTE WELL:  This instruction should be given where, in
a  criminal  trial  involving  multiple  defendants,  one
defendant  pleads  guilty  leaving  jury  issues  between
the State and another defendant.

Members  of  the  jury,  the  matter  of  the  State  of  North  Carolina

versus (name defendant) is no longer for your consideration.  You will not

be required to decide that case.  You will not allow this development to

affect your deliberations in any way neither should it affect your decision

in the case between the State of North Carolina and (name defendant or

defendants).

1. For special problems in capital cases see State v. Hucks, 323 N.C. 574 (1988).
