whether or not for compensation, and whether or not for satisfaction of a

debt, (and that the defendant was not a victim [coerced] [deceived] into

committing the offense of involuntary servitude of a minor), it would be

your duty to return a verdict of guilty.  If you do  not  so  find  or  have  a

reasonable doubt as to one or more of these things, it would be your duty

to return a verdict of not guilty.
