GENERAL CRIMINAL VOLUME

N.C. Gen. Stat. § 20-63(g)
------------------------------

270.62 WILLFULLY COVERING REGISTRATION PLATE. INFRACTION.

The  defendant  has  been  charged  with  willfully  covering  a  registration

plate.

For you to find the defendant responsible for this infraction, the State

must prove the following three things beyond a reasonable doubt.

First, that the defendant was an operator of a motor vehicle.

Second, that  the defendant willfully [covered] [caused to  be covered]

a  part  or  portion  of  a  registration  plate  or  the  figures  or  letters  on  a

registration plate.

And  Third,  that  the  defendant  used  a  device  [designed]  [intended]  to

[prevent]  [interfere  with]  the  taking  of  a  clear  photograph  of  a  registration

plate by a traffic control system using cameras.

If  you  find  from  the  evidence  beyond  a  reasonable  doubt  that  on  or

about the alleged date the defendant was an operator of a motor vehicle and

that  the  defendant  willfully  [covered]  [caused  to  be  covered]  a  part  or

portion of a registration plate or the figures or letters on a registration plate

by  using  a  device  [designed]  [intended]  to  [prevent]  [interfere  with]  the

taking  of  a  clear  photograph  of  a  registration  plate  by  a  traffic  control

system  using  cameras,  it  would  be  your  duty  to  find  the  defendant

responsible.  If  you  do  not  so  find  or  have  a  reasonable  doubt  as  to  one  or

more  of  these  things,  it  would  be  your  duty  to  return  a  verdict  of  not

responsible.
