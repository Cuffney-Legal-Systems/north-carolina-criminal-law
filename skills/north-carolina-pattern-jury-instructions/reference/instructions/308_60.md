4. Use appropriate bracketed statement. In the event that there is a dispute as to
which weapon was used and one of the weapons is non-deadly as a matter of law, e.g., a

5. Serious injury may be defined as #such physical injury as causes great pain and
suffering.$ See S. v. Jones, 258 N.C. 89 (1962), or S. v. Ferguson, 261, N.C. 558 (1964). If
there is evidence as to injuries which could not  conceivably  be  considered  anything  but
serious,  the  trial  judge  may  instruct  the  jury  as  follows:  #(Describe  injury)  would  be  a
serious injury.$ S. v. Davis, 33 N.C. App. 262 (1977).
