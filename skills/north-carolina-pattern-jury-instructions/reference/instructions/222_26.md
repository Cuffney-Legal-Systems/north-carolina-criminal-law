1. N.C. Gen. Stat. § 14-159.12 (e) states “as used in subsections (c) and (d) of this

section, the term ‘facility’ shall mean a building or other infrastructure.”

2. For a definition of agriculture, see N.C. Gen. Stat. § 106-581.1.

3. For a definition of energy facility, see N.C. Gen. Stat. § 14-150.2(a).

4. For a definition of public utility, see N.C. Gen. Stat. § 62-3(23).
