220.34  PATTERN OF RESIDENTIAL MORTGAGE FRAUD.  FELONY.

The  defendant  has  been  charged  with  a  pattern  of  residential

mortgage fraud.

1. A pattern of residential mortgage fraud can also consist of a pattern of receiving

4. N.C. Gen. Stat.   14-118.12(b) states !it shall be sufficient in any prosecution

person or entity was harmed financially in the transaction or that the person or entity to
whom the deliberate misstatement, misrepresentation, or omission was made relied upon the
misstatement, misrepresentation or omission.%
