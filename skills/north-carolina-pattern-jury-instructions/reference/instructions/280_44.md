FACILITY OFFICER.  MISDEMEANOR.

The defendant has been charged with misdemeanor escape.

For you to find the defendant guilty of this offense, the State must

prove two things beyond a reasonable doubt.

First, that the defendant was

(a.)  [lawfully  confined

in  a  [prison]  [jail]  [lock-up]

facility

maintained by a [county] [municipality] of North Carolina]

(b.) [in the lawful custody of a(n) [superintendent] [guard] [officer]

of  any  [prison]  [jail]  [lock-up]  facility  maintained  by  a  [county]

[municipality] of North Carolina]

And  Second,  and  that  the  defendant  [broke  from  such  lawful

confinement] [escaped from such custody].

about the alleged date the defendant was
