206.18  CONSPIRACY TO COMMIT MURDER.  FELONY.

The defendant has been charged with conspiracy to commit murder.

For you to find the defendant guilty of this offense, the State must

prove three things beyond a reasonable doubt:

First, that the defendant and at least one other person entered into

an agreement.1

Second, that the agreement was to commit murder.  Murder is the

unlawful killing of another with malice.

And  Third,  that  the  defendant  and  at  least  one  other  person

intended that the  agreement  be  carried  out  at  the  time  it  was  made.

(The State is not required to prove that the murder was committed.2)

If you find from the evidence beyond a reasonable doubt that on or

about the alleged date, the defendant conspired with another to commit

murder, and that the defendant and at least one other person intended at

that time that the murder be committed, then it would be  your  duty  to

return a verdict of  guilty.    If  you  do  not  so  find,  or  have  a  reasonable

doubt as to one or more of these things, it would be your duty to return a

verdict of not guilty.

1. An agreement may be oral, written, or established by certain acts.

2. S. v. Furr, 292 N.C. 711 (1977).
