------------------------------

104.50A  PHOTOGRAPHS, VIDEO, ETC. - AS SUBSTANTIVE EVIDENCE.

N.C. Gen. Stat.   8-97.1

A [photograph] [video] [motion picture] [x-ray] (other photographic

representation)  was  introduced  into  evidence  in  this  case.    This

[photograph]  [video]  [motion  picture]  [x-ray]  (other  photographic

representation)  may  be  considered  by  you  as  evidence  of  facts  it

illustrates or shows.

1.  N.C.  Gen.  Stat.     8-97  does  not  include  maps  or  models  among  the  items
authorized  to  be  treated  as  substantive  evidence.    Where  they  are  introduced  into
evidence, N.C.P.I.-Crim. 104.50 would still be appropriate.
