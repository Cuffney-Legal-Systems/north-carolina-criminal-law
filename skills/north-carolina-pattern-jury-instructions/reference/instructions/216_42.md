216.42  FELONIOUS [RECEIVING] [POSSESSING] PROPERTY IN THE
CUSTODY OF A LAW ENFORCEMENT AGENCY.  FELONY.

The  defendant  has  been  charged  with

felonious  [receiving]

[possession] property  in  the  custody  of  a  law  enforcement  agency  that

was explicitly represented as stolen to the person by [an agent] [person

authorized to act on the behalf] of the law enforcement agency.

For you to find the defendant guilty of this offense, the State must

prove two things beyond a reasonable doubt:

First,

that

the  defendant  knowingly  [received]  [possessed]

property.

And  Second,  that  the  defendant  [received]  [possessed]  this

property after [an agent] [person  authorized  to  act  on  the  behalf]  of  a

law enforcement agency explicitly represented to the defendant that this

property was stolen.

If you find from the evidence beyond a reasonable doubt that on or

about the alleged  date  the  defendant  knowingly  [received]  [possessed]

property after [an agent] [person  authorized  to  act  on  the  behalf]  of  a

law enforcement agency explicitly represented to the defendant that the

property was stolen, it would be your duty to return a verdict of guilty. If

you do not so find or have a reasonable doubt as to one or both of these

things, it would be your duty to return a verdict of not guilty.
