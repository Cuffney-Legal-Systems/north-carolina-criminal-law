258.30  ERECTING OR MAINTAINING SIGNS ON HIGHWAYS.
MISDEMEANOR.

The  defendant  has  been  charged  with  [erecting]  [maintaining]  a

[warning sign] [direction sign] [marker] [signal] [light] (or) [an imitation

[warning sign] [direction sign] [marker] [signal] [light]] upon a highway.1

For you to find the defendant guilty of this offense, the State must

prove two things beyond a reasonable doubt:

First,  that  the  defendant  [erected]  [maintained]  a  [warning  sign]

[direction sign] [marker] [signal] [light] (or) [an imitation [warning sign]

[direction sign]2 [marker] [signal] [light]] upon a highway;

And  Second,  that  the  defendant  was  not  authorized3  to  [erect]

[maintain]  such  (imitation)  [warning  sign]  [direction  sign]  [marker]

[signal] [light].  If you find from the evidence beyond a reasonable doubt

that on or about the alleged date the defendant, without authorization,

[erected] [maintained] a [warning sign] [direction sign] [marker] [signal]

[light] (or) [an imitation [warning sign] [direction sign] [marker] [signal]

[light]]  upon  a  highway,  it  would  be  your  duty  to  return  a  verdict  of

guilty.  If you do not  so  find  or  have  a  reasonable  doubt  as  to  one  or

more of these things, then it would be your duty to return a verdict of not

guilty.

1. N.C. Gen. Stat.   20-4.01 (13) defines !highway" as ![t]he entire width between
property or right-of-way lines of every way or place of whatever nature, when any part
thereof is open to the use of the public as a matter of right for the purposes of vehicular
traffic. The terms "highway" and "street" and their cognates are synonymous."

2.  N.C.  Gen.  Stat.     136-32  (a)  provides  that  imitation  of  !any  official  sign"  is

prohibited.
