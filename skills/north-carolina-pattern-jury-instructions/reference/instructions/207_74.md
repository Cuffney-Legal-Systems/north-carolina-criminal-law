207.74 FAILING TO [PROVIDE NECESSARY INFORMATION TO] [COOPERATE
WITH GUIDELINES AND REGULATIONS OF] THE DEPARTMENT OF
CORRECTIONS WHILE REQUIRED TO ENROLL IN A SATELLITE-BASED
MONITORING PROGRAM1. MISDEMEANOR.

The  defendant  has  been  charged  with  failing  to  [provide  necessary

information  to]  [cooperate  with  guidelines  and  regulations  of]  the

Department  of  Corrections  while  required  to  enroll  in  a  satellite-based

monitoring program.2

For  you  to  find  the  defendant  guilty  of  this  offense,  the  State  must

prove two things beyond a reasonable doubt:

First,  that  the  defendant  was  required  to  enroll3  in  a  satellite-based

monitoring program.

And  Second,  that  the  defendant  failed  to  [provide  necessary

information (describe necessary information that defendant failed to provide)

to the Department of Corrections] [cooperate with guidelines and regulations

of  the  Department  of  Corrections  by  (describe  guidelines  or  regulations

violated)].

If  you  find  from  the  evidence  beyond  a  reasonable  doubt  that  on  or

about  the  alleged  date  the  defendant  was  required  to  enroll  in  a  satellite-

based  monitoring  program  and  failed  to  [provide  necessary  information  to

the Department of Corrections] [cooperate with guidelines and regulations of

the Department of  Corrections,  it would be your  duty to return a verdict of

guilty. If you do not so find or have a reasonable doubt as to one or both of

these things, it would be your duty to return a verdict of not guilty.

FAILING TO [PROVIDE NECESSARY INFORMATION TO] [COOPERATE WITH GUIDELINES
AND REGULATIONS OF] THE DEPARTMENT OF CORRECTIONS WHILE REQUIRED TO ENROLL
IN A SATELLITE-BASED MONITORING. MISDEMEANOR.
GENERAL CRIMINAL VOLUME
REPLACEMENT JUNE 2008
N.C. Gen. Stat. § 14-208.44(c)
------------------------------

1. Neither “cooperate” nor “necessary information” are defined by the statute.

2. According to G.S. § 14-208.40(c)(1) “satellite-based monitoring program” is a

system that provides “time-correlated and continuous tracking of the geographic location of
the subject using a global positioning system based on satellite and other location tracking
technology.”

3.ß G.S. § 14-208.44(d) states “enroll shall include appearing, as directed by the

Department (of Corrections), to receive the necessary equipment.”
