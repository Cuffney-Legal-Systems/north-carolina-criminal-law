247.20  INSTIGATING OR PROMOTING CRUELTY TO (AN) ANIMAL(S).

The  defendant  has  been  charged  with  willfully  instigating1  cruelty  to

(an) animal(s).

For  you  to  find  the  defendant  guilty  of  this  offense  the  State  must

prove two things beyond a reasonable doubt:

First,  that  the  defendant  [instigated]  [promoted]  an  act  of  cruelty  to

(name animal(s)) by

a.

[overdriving]  [overloading]  [eounding]  [injuring]  [tormenting]2

[killing] [depriving of necessary sustenance]];

b.

[causing] [procuring] to be [overdriven] [overloaded] [wounded]

[injured]

[tormented]3

[killed]

[deprived  of  necessary

sustenance];

c.

[torturing]4

[mutilating]

[maiming]

[cruelly

beating]5

[disfiguring] [poisoning] [killing]];

d.

[causing]  [procured]  to  be  [tortured]6  [mutilated]  [maimed]

[cruelly beat]7 [disfigured] [poisoned] [killed]];

And Second, that the defendant acted willfully, that is intentionally and

without justification and excuse.8

NOTE  WELL:  If  the  defendant  presents  evidence  of  justification
or  excuse,  e.g.,  self-defense,  defense  of  other,  etc.,  then  the
instruction  must  be  amended  to  add  these  defenses.  If  added,
these defenses must be included to the mandate.

If  you  find  from  the  evidence  beyond  a  reasonable  doubt  that  on  or

about the alleged date the defendant willfully [instigated] [promoted] an act

of cruelty to (name type of animal), it would be your duty to return a verdict

of guilty.  If you do not so find or have a reasonable doubt as to one or more
