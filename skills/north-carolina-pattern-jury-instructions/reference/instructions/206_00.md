206.00  FIRST DEGREE MURDER, PREMEDITATION AND DELIBERATION -
SECOND DEGREE MURDER AS LESSER INCLUDED OFFENSE.

State v.  Strickland, 307  N.C.  274,  at  289-91  (1983),  in  overruling

State  v.  Harris,  290  N.C.  718  (1976),  held  that,  when  the  indictment

charges  first  degree  murder  by  premeditation  and  deliberation,  a  trial

judge is required to instruct on second degree murder only if the evidence

tends to show a lack of premeditation and deliberation or would permit a

jury rationally to find  defendant  guilty  of  the  lesser  offense  and  acquit

him of the greater.
