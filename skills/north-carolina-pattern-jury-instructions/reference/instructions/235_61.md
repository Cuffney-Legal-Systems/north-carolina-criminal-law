235.61 UNLAWFUL DISTRIBUTION OF IMAGES TAKEN BY UNMANNED
AIRCRAFT.

The defendant has been charged  with  the  unlawful  distribution  of

images taken by a device attached to an unmanned aircraft system.1

For you to find the defendant guilty of this offense, the State must

prove four things beyond a reasonable doubt.

First,  that  the  defendant  recorded  images  through  the  use  of

[infrared] [thermal imaging] technology.

Second, that the device that recorded  the  image  was  attached  to

an unmanned aircraft system.

Third,  that  the  recorded  images  revealed  [individuals]  [materials]

[activities]  inside  of  a  structure  without  the  consent  of  the  property

owner.

And  Fourth,  that  the  defendant  [published]  [disseminated]  the

recorded images.

If you find from the evidence beyond a reasonable doubt that on or

about the alleged date the defendant recorded images through the use of

[infrared]  [thermal  imaging]  technology  that  was  attached  to  an

unmanned  aircraft  system

that

revealed  [individuals]

[materials]

[activities]  inside  of  a  structure  without  the  consent  of  the  property

owner,  and  the  defendant  [published]  [disseminated]  the  recorded

images, it would be your duty to find the defendant guilty. If you do not

so find or have a reasonable doubt as to one or more of these things, it
