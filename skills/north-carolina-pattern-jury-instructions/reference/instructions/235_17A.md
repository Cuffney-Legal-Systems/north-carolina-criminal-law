235.17A  [CAUSING] [ENCOURAGING] [AIDING] A MINOR TO [CARRY]
[POSSESS] WEAPONS ON EDUCATIONAL PROPERTY.1  N.C. Gen. Stat. § 14-
269.2(c) and (c1).2  CLASS I FELONY, MISDEMEANOR.3

The defendant has been charged with [causing] [encouraging] [aiding]

a minor to [carry] [possess] a weapon on educational property.4

For  you  to  find  the  defendant  guilty  of  this  offense,  the  State  must

prove three things beyond a reasonable doubt:

First,  that  the  defendant  [caused]  [encouraged]  [aided]  a  person  to

[carry]5 [possess]6 a (describe weapon).

Second, that the person was a minor under 18 years of age.

And  Third,  that  the  minor  [carried]  [possessed]  that  weapon  on

educational property.

If  you  find  from  the  evidence  beyond  a  reasonable  doubt  that  on  or

about  the  alleged  date,  the  defendant  [caused]  [encouraged]  [aided]  a

person under 18 years of age to [carry] [possess] a (describe weapon), and

that the minor [carried] [possessed] that weapon on educational property, it

would  be  your  duty  to  return  a  verdict  of  guilty.    If  you  do  not  so  find  or

Gen. Stat. § 14-269.2(g) explains situations where the statute does not apply.  In addition,

2 N.C. Gen. Stat. § 14-269.2(c) deals with firearms and lists examples, punished as

3  See  N.C.  Gen.  Stat.  §  14-269.2(e)  and  (f)  for  a  list  of  weapons  and  situations

statute’s coverage.

5 The statute applies whether or not the weapon is concealed.

6 If constructive possession is an issue in the case refer to N.C.P.I.-Crim. 104.41.
