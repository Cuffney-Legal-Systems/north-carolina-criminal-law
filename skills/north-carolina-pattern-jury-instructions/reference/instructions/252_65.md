252.65  TATTOOING A MINOR.  MISDEMEANOR.

The defendant has been charged with tattooing a minor.

For you to find the defendant guilty of this offense, the State must

prove two things beyond a reasonable doubt:

First, that the defendant tattooed, that is placed an indelible mark

under the skin of the [arm] [limb] [body] of the victim.

And Second,  that  at  that  time,  the  victim  was  under  18  years  of

age.

If you find from the evidence beyond a reasonable doubt that on or

about the alleged date, the defendant tattooed the [arm] [limb] [body] of

the  victim,  who  was  under  18  years  of  age,  it  would  be  your  duty  to

return  a  verdict  of  guilty.    If  you  do  not  so  find  or  have  a  reasonable

doubt as to one or both of these things, it would be your duty to return a

verdict of not guilty.
