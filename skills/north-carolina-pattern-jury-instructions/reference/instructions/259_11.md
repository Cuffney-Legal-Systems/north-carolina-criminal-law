259.11  UNAUTHORIZED PRACTICE OF MEDICINE - PRACTICING WITHOUT
A LICENSE WHILE REPRESENTING ONESELF AS BEING LICENSED.
FELONY.

The defendant has been charged with the unauthorized practice of

medicine.

For you to find the defendant guilty of this offense, the state must

prove three things beyond a reasonable doubt:

First, that the defendant was not duly [licensed] (and) [registered]

to practice medicine in this State;

Second, that the defendant  practiced  [medicine]  [surgery]1 in  this

State;

And  Third,  that  the  defendant

falsely  represented  [himself]

[herself] in a manner as being [licensed] (and) [registered] in this State.

If you find from the evidence beyond a reasonable doubt that on or

about  the  alleged  date  the  defendant  was  not  duly  [licensed]  (and)

[registered]  to  practice  medicine  in  this  State,  that  the  defendant

practiced  [medicine]  [surgery]  in  this  State,  and  that  the  defendant

falsely  represented  [himself]  [herself]  in  a  manner  as  being  [licensed]

(and) [registered] in this State, it would be your duty to return a verdict

of guilty.  If you do not so find or have a reasonable doubt as to one or

more of these things, then it would be your duty to return a verdict of not

guilty.
