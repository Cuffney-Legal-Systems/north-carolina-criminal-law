120.11  DEFINITION OF SERIOUS BODILY INJURY.

NOTE WELL:  Use  this  definition  when   serious  bodily
injury!  as  set  forth  in  N.C.  Gen.  Stat.  "  14-32.4  is
required.  When the requirement is  serious injury! use

Serious  bodily  injury  is  bodily  injury  that  creates  or  causes  [a

substantial  risk  of  death]  [serious  permanent  disfigurement]  [coma]  [a

permanent or protracted condition that causes extreme pain] [permanent

or protracted loss or impairment of the functions of any bodily member or

organ] [prolonged hospitalization].
